Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a household?

Go to the Profile tab and tap "Create Household." You'll receive an invite code to share with your partner. They can join by tapping "Join Household" and entering the code.

How does matching work?

Both you and your household partner swipe on recipe cards independently. When you both swipe right on the same recipe, it's a Match! The matched meal appears in your Meals tab.

What's the difference between Cook Tonight and Plan Week?

Cook Tonight shows only recipes you can make with what's already in your pantry. Plan Week lets you plan meals for specific days of the upcoming week, regardless of pantry contents — a shopping list is generated automatically.

How do I manage my pantry?

The Pantry tab lets you search and add ingredients you have at home. After cooking a meal, Dinder will ask what you ran out of — those items are removed from your pantry and added to your shopping list.

Can I exclude ingredients I don't like?

Yes! In the Profile tab, you can add hated ingredients (like cilantro) or exclude entire categories (like Seafood). Recipes containing those ingredients will never appear in your swipe feed.

How do I delete my account?

Contact us at support@cpmoran.com and we'll process your account deletion within 30 days.
